title: "A garment is assembled from blocks: data decides the parts, one module redraws the waist"
---

Which parts a dress, a skirt or a blouse needs is a small table, so a spec the AI fills with a missing part becomes a bilingual refusal instead of a crash, and an extra part is a warning. A dress with a waist seam keeps two blocks and only lines them up: the skirt dart moves under the bodice dart and notches mark where they meet. A one-piece dress keeps the bodice above the waist and the skirt below the hip and redraws only the zone between, so the waist dart becomes a fisheye and the princess seam runs on to the hem.
