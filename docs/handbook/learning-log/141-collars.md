title: "A collar is drafted from the finished neck, so it fits by construction"
---

Each collar reads the neck the bodice already has and never redraws it. The peter pan traces the front and back necks with a rigid move (mirror, lay on the shoulder, turn for the roll), and a rigid move keeps every length, so its neck edge is the bodice neck. A stand gets its CF curve first and makes the straight part from CB whatever length is left, so it is as long as the half neck without any search. Splitting the collar neck at the shoulder lets each half pair with the bodice piece it sews to, and the notches the collar declares are copied onto the bodice by the finishing step. Combinations the method cannot draft (a collar on a V neck, a closed stand) are refused with a message instead of drawn wrong.
