title: "A0 reserves a header band instead of a cover page"
---

The A0 file is the A4 builder with a different paper and no cover. A cover would cost a whole extra sheet of expensive paper, so the test square and title block sit in a 140 mm band at the top of sheet 1: the band is added to what the tiler sees, and the pieces are drawn shifted back by the same amount. The tile label and the page map appear only when there is more than one sheet.
