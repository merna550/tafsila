title: "Pivot the dart, then true the seam to its partner"
---

Princess seams come from dart manipulation: the bust dart is closed by turning the side panel about a pivot level with the dart, so its legs meet exactly, and the opening becomes the style line. Each side-panel curve is then trued by bisection to the length of its centre-panel partner, so the seam and its bust notch match by construction and the validators only confirm it. A split armhole is described by `armholeParts`, so the sleeve sews to whichever panels hold it.
