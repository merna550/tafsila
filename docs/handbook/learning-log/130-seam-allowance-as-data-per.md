Seam allowance as data per edge: an `Edge.kind` with `fold` meaning "no allowance", one offset of the whole stitch path with a distance per segment, the sign flipped for clockwise paths instead of reversing them, and the mirrored hem corner so a turned-up hem lies on the side seam allowance
