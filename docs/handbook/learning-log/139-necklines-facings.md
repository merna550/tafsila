title: "A facing is an offset, so the neck must never bend tighter than it"
---

Every neckline is a named change of the block neck (deeper, wider) and ends square to the shoulder, so the front and back meet smoothly once the shoulder is sewn. The facing's outer edge is the neck moved 60 mm toward the body, so any bend of the neck toward the body tighter than 60 mm (plus the seam allowance) makes the edge loop. The round family adapts its handles to the depth/width ratio, the V turns one way only, and the sweetheart is built from circular arcs whose radii leave room for the facing; depths that would force a tighter bend are clamped with a warning instead of drawn.
