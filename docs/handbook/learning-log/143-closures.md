title: "A closure is a late transform that finds its edges by name"
---

Every garment module draws its pieces on the fold first; the closure module runs last and changes only what the opening needs. It finds the pieces along the opening by their stable ids and edge names, top to bottom, so a zip-stop notch can land on the skirt even though the length is counted from the neck. A zip turns a fold into a seam and widens its allowance; buttons move the centre line out by the extension and mark the buttons on the old centre line. The extension is an overlap, not body, so the body lines declare it and the circumference check leaves it out instead of failing.
