Measurement screens: a form of 18 typed boxes where the engine checks on every change (errors block, warnings do not), a collapsible group that keeps its values by hiding instead of unmounting, a modal guide with placeholder line art drawn from JSON data, and tests that fail when a field has no guide text ([decision 31b](../../decisions/31b-measurement-screens.md))
