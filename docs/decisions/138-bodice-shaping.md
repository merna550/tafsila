# ADR-138 · 2026-10-18 · Princess seams (shoulder, armhole) and the dart-free bodice

**Context:** Issue #138 (FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-01, -03, -04, -08). Plan: [`docs/plans/138-bodice-shaping.md`](../plans/138-bodice-shaping.md). Method: "Princess seams and dart-free bodice" in [`drafting-method.md`](../engine/drafting-method.md).
**Decision:**

1. **Pivot.** The side-seam bust dart is closed by turning the upper side of the front about a pivot at the bust point's x, half way between the dart legs. The block's legs share their x and sit symmetric about that line (decision 128, 2), so the turned upper leg lands exactly on the lower one and the side seam keeps its length. The pivot is a few mm above or below the bust point; the princess line passes through it and the bust notch sits there.
2. **Style line start.** Shoulder variant: half way along the shoulder (`PRINCESS_SHOULDER_SHARE` 0.5), the back at the same distance from its neck point so the shoulder seams pair panel to panel. Armhole variant: 0.35 of the armhole from the shoulder tip, but always leaving 110 mm below it (`PRINCESS_ARMHOLE_MIN_LOWER_MM`) so the sleeve balance notches stay on the side panel.
3. **Curves and truing.** Each stretch of the seam is one cubic, square to the bust level at the pivot (front) or the waist dart apex (back). The front waist dart legs are re-trued about the pivot. The centre panel uses the Catmull-Rom handle (1/3 of the chord); the side panel's handle is found by bisection so its length equals the centre panel's: FR-VAL-01 and FR-VAL-04 come out at 0 mm on every matrix body, and nothing is forced (an unreachable target is reported, not hidden). The back has no bust dart, so its panels share the upper stretch and the lower stretches mirror each other.
4. **Pieces.** `bodice-front-centre` and `bodice-back-centre` (cut 1 on the fold), `bodice-front-side` and `bodice-back-side` (cut 2, mirrored) replace `bodice-front` / `bodice-back`. Edges: `neck`, `shoulder`, `armhole`, `style` (paired with the other panel, reversed), `side`, `waist`, `cf` / `cb`. No darts remain. Bust line: horizontal at the pivot on the centre front, the polyline pivot → closed dart point on the side front, horizontal at the underarm at the back; waist line: the `waist` edges.
5. **Sleeve with a split armhole.** `armholeParts(pieces)` (in `blocks/bodice`) lists the pieces holding each armhole from the shoulder tip down. The sleeve puts its balance notches on the part at the underarm and the shoulder notch on the part at the tip; its cap edges pair with the armhole only when it is one edge, otherwise FR-VAL-02 checks the cap ease over all parts.
6. **Dart-free (`none`).** The waist dart already goes to the side seam (decision 128, 7). The bust dart is removed: the front side waist moves to the point whose side seam equals the back's and whose waist edge keeps the block's length (about one dart opening higher), so FR-VAL-01 and FR-VAL-03 hold and the intake hangs as length ease at the centre front. `bodice/none` reports FR-VAL-08 `BODICE_DART_FREE_OK` (pass) for loose and semi-fitted and `BODICE_DART_FREE_FITTED` (warn) for fitted: the pattern is drawn, but never claimed as fitted.
7. **`ENGINE_VERSION` 0.7.0 → 0.8.0:** princess specs used to get the darted block, `none` kept the bust dart; every snapshot carries the new stamp.

**Consequences:** Both princess variants and the dart-free bodice draft and validate across the size matrix, every fit and ease profile, with and without set-in sleeves. `needs:sew-test` stays on #138; toiles of each princess variant in 3 sizes are tracked in #208 and logged in `docs/test-cases/engine/sew-tests.md`.
