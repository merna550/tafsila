# ADR-139 · 2026-10-18 · Necklines, front depth override and neck facings

**Context:** Issue #139 (FR-PAT-05, FR-PAT-07, FR-VAL-01, -05, -08). Plan: [`docs/plans/139-necklines-facings.md`](../plans/139-necklines-facings.md). Method: "Necklines and facings" in [`drafting-method.md`](../engine/drafting-method.md).
**Decision:**

1. **Depth.** Each style is a named change of the block neck (`FRONT_NECKLINE_SIZES`, `BACK_NECKLINE_SIZES` in `constants/neckline.ts`): `depthAddMm` on the block depth and `widenMm` along the shoulder. Depth is measured down the centre line from the level of the (moved) neck point. `depthFrontMm` replaces the front style depth. Both halves are clamped to [minimum, 30 mm above the armscye line and, at the front, the bust point]: the front minimum is 40 mm (120 mm for the sweetheart, which needs room for its lobe; 100 mm for the square, whose turn into the shoulder gets too tight for the facing below it), the back 10 mm. A clamped depth is drafted at the limit and reported as an FR-VAL-08 warning (`NECK_FRONT_DEPTH_CLAMPED`, `NECK_BACK_DEPTH_CLAMPED`) that names the asked and drafted depths; inside the limits the module reports `NECK_DEPTH_OK`.
2. **Width.** The front and back neck points move the same distance along their shoulders, so the shoulder seam keeps its length match (FR-VAL-01): the larger of the two styles' `widenMm` (a boat front widens the back too). The widening is held so every piece holding the neck keeps 15 mm of shoulder (a princess-shoulder centre panel is the short one), with an FR-VAL-08 warning `NECK_WIDTH_CLAMPED`.
3. **Curves.** Every neck runs from the centre line to the neck point and ends square to the shoulder, so the front and back necks meet in one smooth line once the shoulder is sewn. Round, high-round and boat: one cubic square to the centre line, its handles adapted to the depth/width ratio so the tightest bend stays above the neck allowance. V: one cubic, straight from the centre toward its last handle (so it turns one way only). Square: level to a corner under the last handle, then up. Sweetheart: circular arcs (dip to lobe, R 110 mm; the turn up the strap, R ≥ 25 mm; into the neck point, R 100 mm) joined by a straight strap, because the facing's outer edge is an offset of the neck and loops on any bend tighter than the facing width plus its allowance. The neckline module owns the sewn neck edge; collars (#141) read it.
4. **Facings.** One facing per half (`neck-facing-front`, `neck-facing-back`, cut 1 on the fold, main fabric) and one interfacing piece with the same outline (`-interfacing`, fabric `interfacing`, no pairs). Outline: the neck itself, the shoulder for the facing width, the outer edge and the fold. The outer edge moves each neck segment 60 mm toward the body (ends along their normals, handles scaled by the change of radius: exact on arcs, well inside 1 mm elsewhere, a few cubics instead of the dozens the seam-allowance offset makes); it lands on the shoulder because the neck meets it square, a corner of the square neck is bridged by an arc, and a V or sweetheart edge is extended to the fold. The facing `neck` pairs with the bodice `neck` and the facing shoulders pair with each other, so the core seam rule checks them within 2 mm. The outer edge is kind `other` (15 mm allowance, neatened, not sewn). The width shrinks only if the shoulder is shorter than 60 mm + 10 mm.
5. **Head opening.** When nothing opens at the neck (`closure.type` `none`, or a side zip), the whole neck seam (2 × front + 2 × back neck) must reach the head circumference, estimated as 1.55 × neck; otherwise FR-VAL-08 warns `NECK_HEAD_OPENING_TIGHT` (else `NECK_HEAD_OPENING_OK`). A centre-back or centre-front closure skips the check.
6. **Pipeline.** `neckline` module, order 10: after the bodice shaping modules (5) decide which piece holds the neck, before the sleeve (30). It splices the new neck into `bodice-front` / `bodice-back` or the princess centre panels (same edge ids and pairs; the grain line stays below the new neck) and appends the four facing pieces. What it resolved is written to `resolved` (`neckline.*`) for its rules.
7. **`ENGINE_VERSION` 0.8.0 → 0.9.0:** every design with a neckline now gets the style curve and four facing pieces; every snapshot carries the new stamp.

**Consequences:** Every front × back combination drafts closed, simple stitch and cut lines across the size matrix on every bodice shaping, and random bodies with random overrides never throw. `needs:sew-test` stays on #139; the toiles (3 necklines in 3 sizes) are tracked in the sew-test follow-up issue and logged in `docs/test-cases/engine/sew-tests.md`.
