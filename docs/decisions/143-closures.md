# ADR-143 · 2026-10-19 · Closures: zip (centre back, side, centre front), buttons and none

**Context:** Issue #143 (FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-04, FR-VAL-08). Plan: [`docs/plans/143-closures.md`](../plans/143-closures.md). Cheap to reverse: every number is a named constant in `packages/engine/src/constants/closure.ts`, the allowed combinations are one table, and the module is one entry in the default registry. Status: accepted, pending sew-test.
**Decision:**

1. **A late transform, with the combinations as data.** The `closure` module runs at order 50, after every module that draws a shell or facing piece, and works on pieces by stable id (`bodice-{half}`, the princess panel holding the edge, `skirt-{half}`, `tier-n-{half}`, or a split band's `-panel`) and edge id (`cb`, `cf`, `side`, panel `seam-left` / `seam-right`), never by path index; a missing edge fails with `CLOSURE_TARGET_MISSING` and its refs. `CLOSURE_MATRIX` allows a zip at every position, buttons at the centre back or front, and `none` everywhere; buttons down a centre line a skirt slit opens, or on a band split into panels, are refused. Refusals are the input error `CLOSURE_UNSUPPORTED` (bilingual `fail`, no pieces, decision 128). `none` opens nowhere whatever its position (`closureOpensAt`), so a `none` + centre-back skirt is now cut on the fold.
2. **Zip.** At the centre back or front the shell pieces and the facings on that line (neck, traced waist, contour band) go from fold to seam: cut as a mirrored pair, the shell seam paired with its own mirror. A side zip uses the front side seam paired with the back (the left side when worn). Every zip seam gets `ZIP_SEAM_ALLOWANCE_MM` (20) through the per-edge allowance table, unless the user set that edge. Zip lengths (`ZIP_LENGTH_MM`): dress 560 at the centres, 400 at the side; skirt 180; a blouse ends at the waist, so its zip runs the whole opening (open-ended, rounded down to 10 mm) with no stop notch. A shorter zip gets a `zip-stop` notch at its length from the top of the opening (down the bodice and into the skirt), paired so FR-VAL-04 checks both edges within 2 mm; a zip that does not stop `ZIP_END_CLEARANCE_MM` (50) above the bottom is `CLOSURE_ZIP_TOO_LONG`. The zip is listed as a notion (`kind: zip`).
3. **Button extension.** Every piece on the centre line (shell and fold facings) gets the centre edge moved out by `BUTTON_EXTENSION_MM` (15, one button diameter) and becomes a mirrored pair. The moved line keeps the edge id (`cf`, `cb`, `fold`); the square ends are their own edges (`-top`, `-bottom`) with the kind of the edge they continue, so the cut line runs on at the neck or hem allowance. Body lines that cross the extension declare `overlapMm`, which the measure leaves out, so FR-VAL-03 still checks the body.
4. **Facing.** Each extended shell piece gets a traced facing `<piece>-facing` (and `-interfacing`): the outline within `FRONT_FACING_WIDTH_MM` (60) of the centre line, extension included, closed by a straight inner edge. Its extension edge pairs with the shell's; its other edges are `along-<shell edge>` so it never answers to the shell's ids (the waistband finds the skirt by `waist`). Under a collarless neck the neck facing also carries the extension and laps over the top of the facing; the sew-test confirms this or a later issue merges the two.
5. **Button marks.** Buttons (15 mm) run from `BUTTON_FIRST_FROM_TOP_MM` (20) below the top of the opening to `BUTTON_LAST_FROM_BOTTOM_MM` (60) above the hem or waist, the fewest that keep every gap within `BUTTON_MAX_SPACING_MM` (90), evenly spaced across bodice and skirt. Each is a `button` point mark on the centre line and a horizontal `buttonhole` line mark starting 3 mm past it towards the edge, button + 3 mm long; the words say which half takes which (holes on the right). The buttons are a notion (`kind: button`). The open wrist cuff of #142 gets one 10 mm button on the underlap and its buttonhole in the 20 mm overlap (decision 142, 5).
6. **None.** Nothing changes in the geometry. A dress, or a skirt the waistband module does not finish, warns (FR-VAL-08 `CLOSURE_HIP_OPENING_TIGHT`) when its narrowest waist line is below hip + `HIP_PASS_EASE_MM`; the waistband (#140) and neck (#139) rules already cover the other cases.
7. **Schema.** `BodyLine.overlapMm`, mark kinds `button` and `buttonhole`, notion kinds `zip` and `button` (additive; JSON Schema regenerated).
8. **`ENGINE_VERSION` 0.12.0 → 0.13.0:** zip and button designs now change their pieces.
