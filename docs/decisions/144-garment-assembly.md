# ADR-144 · 2026-10-19 · Garment assembly: dress with or without a waist seam, category parts

**Context:** Issue #144 (FR-PAT-01, FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-01..07). Plan: [`docs/plans/144-garment-assembly.md`](../plans/144-garment-assembly.md). Cheap to reverse: the category parts are one table (`pipeline/category-rules.ts`), the numbers are named constants in `packages/engine/src/constants/assembly.ts`, and the assembly is two entries in the default registry. Refines [decision 125](125-pipeline.md), 3: a spec missing a part its category needs is now an input error, not a throw. Status: accepted, pending sew-test (#223).
**Decision:**

1. **Category parts as data.** `CATEGORY_PARTS`: a dress needs bodice and skirt and ignores a waistband; a skirt needs a skirt and ignores bodice, neckline and sleeve; a blouse needs a bodice and ignores skirt and waistband. A missing part is the input error `SPEC_PART_MISSING`, and a one-piece dress with a skirt it cannot join is `DRESS_ONE_PIECE_SKIRT_UNSUPPORTED` (bilingual `fail`, no pieces, decision 128, 10). Ignored parts give one FR-VAL-08 `warn` (`SPEC_PART_IGNORED`, refs name the parts) and are dropped before drafting; a `none` waistband is not warned about. The input hash is the spec as given. `bodice.waistSeam` applies to a dress only.
2. **A one-piece dress takes a straight or A-line skirt** (`ONE_PIECE_SKIRT_TYPES`): circle, gathered, pleated and tiered skirts have nothing to join without a seam.
3. **Dress with a waist seam.** The bodice publishes where each waist dart closes on the waist (`bodice.waistDart{Front,Back}FromCentreMm`, distance from CF/CB to the centre-side leg); for a dress the skirt dart's centre-side leg goes there, keeping its own intake (the A-line pivot follows). A paired `waist-dart` notch marks the meeting on a darted bodice over a darted skirt half. Rule `DRESS_WAIST_SEAM_LENGTH` (FR-VAL-01, one row per half) compares the bodice and skirt waists, darts closed, gathers drawn up, pleats folded, on one side of the body, within 2 mm; it reports, never fixes.
4. **One-piece dress.** Module `assembly-one-piece` (order 45, after skirts, sleeves and the waistband, before the closure) keeps the bodice above the waist and the skirt below the hip line (moved so its waist centre sits on the bodice's), and redraws the zone between: the side seam is one curve from the bodice side waist to the skirt's hip side point (cut at the side end of the skirt's hip line, so a pivoted A-line keeps its hip line); a waist dart becomes a fisheye (upper half as drafted, lower half to an apex on the legs' bisector at the skirt dart length, `FISHEYE_HIP_CLEARANCE_MM` above the hip); a princess seam runs from its waist leg to the dart axis on the hip line, then straight down to the hem, as its own edge `style-lower`; with no waist dart, the side seam takes all the shaping. The front lower half is dropped until both side seams match (fixed point, 0.01 mm). Fisheye halves have `edgeId: "interior"`; the waist body line runs along the old waist row and leaves the closed fisheye out (`overlapMm`).
5. **Blouse.** Stays at waist length in this PR; hip length (`BLOUSE_LENGTH_BELOW_HIP_MM` 0, hip row from `hipQuarterMm`) moves to #224 with the robustness work for extreme bodies. There is no tuck-in/over field until the spec has one.
6. **Piece ids stay `bodice-*`**; the skirt pieces are removed. Printed names follow the garment: a one-piece dress shows "أمام الفستان / Dress front"…; a waist-seam dress keeps "bodice" and "skirt".
7. **No SQL seed** until the `designs` table exists: `CATEGORY_SEEDS` in `@tafsila/test-fixtures` (dress `seedDress`, skirt `straightBandSkirt`, blouse `dartedBlouse`) is the seed data, plus `onePieceDress` and `princessOnePieceDress`.
8. `ENGINE_VERSION` 0.14.0: dress skirt darts move under the bodice darts, and conflicting specs no longer throw.
