# ADR-142 · 2026-10-19 · Sleeve styles (cap, puff, bishop, flutter) and cuffs (band, elastic)

**Context:** Issue #142 (FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-01, -02). Plan: [`docs/plans/142-sleeve-styles-cuffs.md`](../plans/142-sleeve-styles-cuffs.md). Cheap to reverse: every number is a named constant in `packages/engine/src/constants/sleeve-style.ts`, `constants/cuff.ts` and `constants/elastic.ts`, and the two modules are two entries in the default registry. Status: accepted, pending sew-test.
**Decision:**

1. **Allowed combinations as data, refused as input errors.** `SLEEVE_COMBINATIONS` lists, per type, the lengths and cuffs it allows: set-in takes every length, a band from short down and elastic only at short and elbow (a straight wrist casing cannot pass the hand); cap and flutter take cap or short with no cuff; puff takes cap, short or elbow with any cuff; bishop takes three-quarter or long and needs a band or elastic. `sleeveCombinationIssue(spec)` (exported for the AI and chat layers) returns `SLEEVE_LENGTH_UNSUPPORTED` or `SLEEVE_CUFF_UNSUPPORTED`; the modules throw it as an input error, so `generatePattern` returns a bilingual `fail` with no pieces (decision 128). A set-in sleeve with a band, ignored until now, is now drafted.
2. **Puff and bishop are reshaped set-in blocks.** The puff head is redrawn between the upper back notch (96 mm from the underarm) and the front notch (75 mm), through two top points `PUFF_CAP_TOP_WIDTH_SHARE` × bicep apart, with the block's tangents at the notches; its rise is solved by bisection so the head is exactly `PUFF_CAP_GATHER_RATIO` (1.5) × the block's (a geometric equivalent of slash-and-spread, with no hinge drift). The cap below the notches, and so the notches, the underarm and FR-VAL-04, are unchanged. With a cuff, the hem is widened to the cuff seam × ratio (puff 1.5; bishop the skirt's 1.8 for the default fabric weight) with straight underarm seams, and dropped 20 mm (puff) or 40 mm (bishop) for the blouson. A bishop keeps the set-in cap.
3. **Cap sleeve and flutter are sewn notch to notch.** The cap sleeve is the block cap from the back notch (90 mm) to the front notch (75 mm), closed by a curved hem through the block hem line, self-lined (cut four). The flutter is a half-circle ruffle whose inner arc is the armhole span plus the set-in cap ease. Both carry the shoulder notch; the bodice keeps its shoulder notch and gets a `sleeve-end` point mark at each balance-notch position (a notch there would have no partner and fail FR-VAL-04). The armhole below is bound (#146).
4. **Declared ease for FR-VAL-02, never a silent fix.** `Piece.sleeveCap` gains `gatheredMm` (cap length gathered on purpose: the puff head) and `armholeExcludedMm` (armhole not sewn to this cap: 165 mm for the cap sleeve and flutter). The rule measures cap − gathered − (armhole − excluded) against 10–40 mm, so set-in and bishop caps are checked as before. Puff cap halves declare their gathers in `intendedEaseMm` to the armhole (FR-VAL-01) and carry `gather` marks.
5. **Band cuff.** Finished length = arm girth at the hem + `CUFF_BAND_EASE_MM` (20); finished width 50 mm at the wrist, 30 mm on the arm; cut folded, twice, with an interfacing. At three-quarter and long the band opens with a 20 mm overlap (pairs with nothing; buttons come with #143), a 75 mm `slit` mark on the back half of the hem and a 150 × 30 mm placket strip, cut twice; above the elbow it is a loop. The arm girth is the upper arm at the underarm line, the wrist at the wrist, linear between (no elbow girth is measured).
6. **Elastic cuff.** A folded loop casing as long as the relaxed hem, 2 × (10 + 5) mm high, cut twice; the elastic is a notion (`sleeve-elastic`, arm girth × `ELASTIC_LENGTH_RATIO` 0.9, 10 mm wide, quantity 2). `ELASTIC_LENGTH_RATIO` and `CASING_EASE_MM` move to `constants/elastic.ts`, shared with the waist.
7. **The cuff matches the hem as declared.** The cuff seam pairs with the sleeve hem and declares cuff − hem as intended ease; the hem carries a `gather` mark to the cuff seam (band). `CUFF_LENGTH_OK / CUFF_LENGTH_MISMATCH` (FR-VAL-01, ± 2 mm) compares the cuff seam with the hem after its gathers, naming the overlap. The waist strip moved to `pieces/strip.ts` and serves both.
8. **Every number is a named constant, pending sew-test** (`needs:sew-test`; toiles of puff and bishop × size-s, size-m, size-xxl are rows in `docs/test-cases/engine/sew-tests.md`).
9. **`ENGINE_VERSION` 0.11.0 → 0.12.0:** set-in designs with a cuff now get cuffs, and cap, puff, bishop and flutter designs, which drew no sleeve before, now get one.
