# ADR-80 · 2026-10-11 · Brand applied: tokens generated from `docs/design-system/tokens.json`

**Context:** ADR-031 kept every brand value in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css` as a PLACEHOLDER until Merna chose the brand. She approved it (palette, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, type scale, radii, shadows). The source of the brand is `docs/design-system/tokens.json` with its `README.md`.
**Decision:** (1) `scripts/gen-tokens.mjs` (`pnpm tokens`) writes the brand part of `tokens.css` (colours, fonts, `--text-*`, radii, shadows) between `BEGIN/END GENERATED` markers; hand-written parts (z-index, motion, spacing, mono font) stay outside. `pnpm tokens:check` fails when the file is out of date and runs in the `quality` CI job. (2) The existing role names stay (`background`, `surface`, `text`, `muted`, `border`, ...), mapped from the approved tokens; new roles `line`, `primary-soft`, `accent`, `on-accent`, `accent-soft`, `saffron`; `--color-secondary*` removed. `primary-hover` (`#0A5651`) is not in tokens.json, so it is a constant in the generator, covered by the contrast test. (3) The Tailwind text scale is reset to the brand scale (xs 13, sm 15, base 17, lg 20, xl 24, 2xl 32); sizes above `2xl` no longer exist. (4) Secondary buttons are outlined; shadows are `shadow-card` and `shadow-overlay`. (5) IBM Plex Sans Arabic loads through `next/font/google` in the web app (variable `--font-plex`) and through `@fontsource/ibm-plex-sans-arabic` in Storybook. (6) `scripts/design-tokens-contrast.test.mjs` asserts every allowed text pair is at least 4.5:1 and control borders and the focus ring at least 3:1.
**Reason:** One source of truth for the brand that a designer can edit as data; CI catches drift; accessibility is tested, not assumed.
**Alternatives:** Hand-editing `tokens.css` (drifts from the brand source); a style-dictionary dependency (heavy for one CSS output).

Replaces the PLACEHOLDER clause of ADR-031; the rest of ADR-031 stands.
