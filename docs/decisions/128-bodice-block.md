# ADR-128 · 2026-10-17 · Bodice block

**Context:** Issue #128 (FR-PAT-01, FR-PAT-02, FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-01, -03, -07, NFR-01). The first real block. While planning, three steps of the #122 reference broke acceptance criteria: the front shoulder was 1.3-2.3 mm longer than the back (over the 2 mm seam tolerance for broad shoulders, and backwards for Aldrich), the bust dart legs differed by 3.7-8.2 mm with the apex at the bust point, and the front waist dart legs differed by up to 2.7 mm on the sloping waist line. All choices below are cheap to reverse: a constant or a step of the reference calculation, then regenerate.

**Decision:**

1. **Equal shoulders.** The back shoulder = front shoulder length + `BACK_SHOULDER_EASE_MM` (0), laid along the back slope; the front tip stays at SW / 2. The ease is declared on the back shoulder edge (`pairsWith.intendedEaseMm`) so FR-VAL-01 reads it. The toile decides whether it becomes 5-7 mm.
2. **Bust dart apex** on the horizontal through the middle of the dart mouth, `BUST_DART_APEX_SETBACK_MM` (25) beyond the bust point towards the side seam. The legs are equal by construction and the apex stays clear of the waist dart.
3. **Front waist dart truing:** the shorter leg is extended along its own line to the longer length (`trueDartLegs`).
4. **The reference changes with the method.** `reference-drafts-calc.ts` and `drafting-method.md` carry decisions 1-3 and the fixture is regenerated; size M was recomputed in a separate one-off script (PR of #128). The engine is never tested against points it cannot meet.
5. **Curves.** Armhole guide point on the 45 degree diagonal from the corner of the width point x and the armscye line: 25 mm at the back, 20 mm at the front (common block practice; Merna checks the book). The armhole is square to the shoulder at the tip, square to the diagonal at the guide point and square to the side seam at the underarm. Tip to guide point is a Catmull-Rom curve; guide point to underarm is one explicit cubic whose first handle is never longer than the diagonal, because a Catmull-Rom curve dipped below the armscye line for some bodies. The neck is one explicit cubic, square to the centre line and to the shoulder, with a centre handle of `NECK_CURVE_HANDLE_RATIO` (0.55) of the neck width and a shoulder handle dropping `NECK_SHOULDER_HANDLE_SHARE` (0.8) of the neck depth: the Catmull-Rom neck dipped below the back nape (up to 0.53 mm), and a 0.55 shoulder handle bent the back neck so tightly that the 10 mm seam allowance of #130 crossed itself. Key points are unchanged by either choice.
6. **Bust circumference** for FR-VAL-03 is measured on the armscye line, where the method sets the bust width.
7. **A waist dart narrower than `MIN_WAIST_DART_MM` (6) is dropped**, and so are waist darts for `bodice.shaping: "none"`. The side seam then takes all the shaping, even when it flares out because the waist quarter is wider than the bust quarter. The bust dart stays (it balances the front length).
8. **Bodice pieces are cut on the fold at CF and CB.** The closure module (#143) turns the CB into a seam.
9. **Blocks are a pipeline step, not `FeatureModule`s.** `BLOCK_DRAFTERS` is keyed by `BlockName`; the skirt entry is an identity step until #129. The block registers no FR-VAL rules: #133 builds them with `pieces/measure.ts`, this issue proves the properties in tests.
10. **Input-type `DraftingError`s become a `fail` result** (`FR-MEAS-01` check with the code and an ar/en message from `INPUT_ERROR_MESSAGES`): `SKIRT_LENGTH_OUT_OF_BODY`, `SKIRT_LENGTH_INVALID_BODY` and the three `BODICE_*` codes. Programming errors (`PIPELINE_STEP_ORDER`, `PIPELINE_UNSUPPORTED_COMBINATION`, geometry codes) still throw.
11. **`DraftContext.inputs`** holds `ResolvedDraftInputs`; `resolved` holds the numbers blocks pass on (`bodice.armholeBackMm`, `bodice.armholeFrontMm`, `bodice.scyeDepthMm`).
12. **The engine's method constants duplicate `DRAFT_CONSTANTS` on purpose** (an independent reference) and a sync test keeps them equal.

**Consequences:** `ENGINE_VERSION` 0.2.0: a dress or blouse now gives two bodice pieces. Merna checks against her copy of Aldrich (6th ed.): the three reference changes (TC-ENG-128-07), the armhole guide distances 25/20 mm, dropping a waist dart under 6 mm, cutting the back on the fold, and the Arabic error messages. The toiles (TC-ENG-128-08) decide the back shoulder ease.
