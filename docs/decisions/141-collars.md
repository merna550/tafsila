# ADR-141 · 2026-10-19 · Collars: peter pan, shirt (stand and fall) and mandarin

**Context:** Issue #141 (FR-PAT-05, FR-VAL-01, -04). Plan: [`docs/plans/141-collars.md`](../plans/141-collars.md). Cheap to reverse: every number is a named constant in `packages/engine/src/constants/collar.ts` (and `BUTTON_EXTENSION_MM` in `constants/closure.ts`, shared with #143), and the module is one entry in the default registry. Status: accepted, pending sew-test.
**Decision:**

1. **Allowed combinations, refused as input errors.** `collarCombinationIssue(spec)` (exported from the engine for the AI and chat layers) returns `COLLAR_NECKLINE_UNSUPPORTED` when the front or back neck is not `round` / `high-round`, `COLLAR_DEPTH_UNSUPPORTED` for a shirt or mandarin with `depthFrontMm` (a stand sits on the natural neck), and `COLLAR_CLOSURE_UNSUPPORTED` for a shirt without a centre-front opening or a mandarin without a CF or CB opening. The module throws `DraftingError(code)`; the codes are in `INPUT_ERROR_MESSAGES`, so `generatePattern` returns a `fail` result with a bilingual message and no pieces (the mechanism of decision 128; no pipeline change). A peter pan takes any closure; the neckline's head-opening rule covers it.
2. **The opening follows the closure.** CF opening (and every peter pan without a CB opening): each collar piece is cut on the CB fold. CB opening on a mandarin: cut on the CF fold, open and rounded at CB. Peter pan with a CB opening: a mirrored pair, rounded at CF and CB.
3. **The neck edge is split at the shoulder and paired.** `neck-back` (CB to shoulder) pairs with the back holder's `neck`, `neck-front` (shoulder to CF) with the front holder's `neck` (`reversed`), so the core seam rule checks each half within 2 mm. The collar carries notches at CB, both shoulders and CF with `pairsWith`; the finishing notch step writes their partners on the bodice necks (FR-VAL-04). The bodice outline is untouched. A whole-neck `COLLAR_NECK_LENGTH_*` check (FR-VAL-01) reports collar neck against bodice neck, front plus back.
4. **Stand collars replace the neck facings; peter pan keeps them.** The inner stand encloses the neck seam, so the neckline module skips its four facing pieces for `shirt` and `mandarin` (`neckHasFacings`). Under a peter pan the facing sandwiches the collar.
5. **Button extension.** Shirt and mandarin stands run on `BUTTON_EXTENSION_MM` (15 mm) past CF, corners rounded 10 mm, when the closure is CF buttons; the `extension` edge pairs with nothing until #143 adds the bodice extension and buttonholes. A CF zip gets no extension.
6. **The stand neck equals the half neck by construction.** The CF curve takes the front neck's horizontal run and the rise; the straight part from CB is the rest of the half neck, so no iteration is needed (the plan's bisection was replaced, see the plan's Deviations). The shirt fall's neck edge is a copy of the stand top, so the fall-to-stand seam is equal by construction too.
7. **Peter pan roll and turn of cloth.** The back neck is mirrored on its shoulder, laid on the front shoulder at the neck point and turned so the tips overlap 13 mm; the outer edge is the neck offset 70 mm, the two halves blended over 30 mm where they meet above the shoulder (so the seam allowance never crosses itself). The under collar (and the shirt's under fall) is 3 mm smaller; the outer seam declares the difference as `intendedEaseMm`.
8. **Every number is a named constant, pending sew-test** (`needs:sew-test`; toiles of 3 collars × size-s, size-m, size-xxl are rows in `docs/test-cases/engine/sew-tests.md`).
9. **`ENGINE_VERSION` 0.10.0 → 0.11.0:** designs with a collar now get collar pieces and stand-collar designs lose their facings; every snapshot carries the new stamp.
